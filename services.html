Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to common questions regarding timelines, materials, and warranty coverage.

Most standard 2-car garages are completely transformed in 1 to 2 days. Because we pre-engineer all steel modules off-site and use ultra-fast curing polyaspartic coatings, you can walk on the floor in 6 hours and park your vehicle in 24 hours.

Standard epoxy dries brittle, yellowing in sunlight and peeling when hot tires rest on it. Our industrial polyaspartic coating penetrates deep into the concrete capillaries via diamond grinding, flexing under thermal stress and resisting 100% of automotive chemicals.

Our heavy-duty steel cabinet units are rated for up to 1,000 lbs per freestanding module, with individual drawer slides rated at 140 lbs. Our motorized ceiling lifts support up to 800 lbs of distributed load safely anchored to primary ceiling joists.

Our warranty guarantees against cabinet hinge failure, structural deflection, floor peeling/delamination, and slatwall breakage under normal use. It is fully transferable if you sell your property, adding tangible appraisal value to your home.
